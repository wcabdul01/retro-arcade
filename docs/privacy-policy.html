Retro Arcade — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Retro Arcade ("the App") is a collection of classic arcade-style games (Brick Breaker, Block Drop, Block Rise, Snake, Tank War, Racing, Star Defender, Memory Match, Sudoku, and Solitaire). This page explains what data the App collects and how it's used.

Data We Collect

  • Game data (high scores, progress, settings). Saved entirely on your device using local storage. This data is never transmitted to us or to any server we control.

This version of the App does not display ads and does not collect or use your device's advertising identifier. A future update is planned to add ads through a third-party ad network (AppLovin); this policy will be updated to describe that network's data practices before any such update ships.

Third-Party Services

This version of the App does not use any third-party advertising or analytics services.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Sharing & Sale

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with any third parties.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

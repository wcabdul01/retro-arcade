Retro Arcade — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Retro Arcade ("the App") is a collection of classic arcade-style games (Brick Breaker, Block Drop, Block Rise, Snake, Tank War, Racing, Star Defender, Memory Match, Sudoku, and Solitaire). This page explains what data the App collects and how it's used.

Data We Collect

  • Game data (high scores, progress, settings). Saved on your device using local storage. This data is never transmitted to us or to any server we control. On Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of your high scores and level progress is also stored in your Google Play Games account (see Cloud Save below).
  • Purchase status. Whether you have bought the optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase, as reported by the app store.

We (the developer) do not collect any personal data ourselves.

Advertising

The Android version of the App shows ads from Unity, using Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads (services of Unity Technologies). To serve, measure and protect ads, Unity collects and processes:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as your Android advertising ID)
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address (not GPS)
  • Interactions with ads (views, taps, ad completion)
  • Device and app information and diagnostics (device model, OS version, language, ad performance)

This data is encrypted in transit and used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Unity's handling of it is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, which also explains how to request access to or deletion of that data.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Watching optional "Watch Ad" videos is always your choice. The iOS version of the App does not show ads.

In-App Purchases

The App offers one optional, one-time purchase, "Remove Ads", which turns off all ads and ad-based rewards. It is sold through Google Play Billing (Google Play version) or Huawei In-App Purchases (AppGallery version). Payment is handled entirely by Google or Huawei: we never see or store your card or payment details. The store tells the App only whether you own the purchase, along with an order reference used to confirm it, so the purchase can be restored after a reinstall or on a new device. How Google and Huawei handle payment data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Huawei Privacy Statement.

Cloud Save (Google Play Games)

On the Google Play version, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, the App saves your high scores and level progress to Google Play Games Saved Games, so they come back after a reinstall or on a new device. This data is stored by Google in your Play Games account under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive it on any server of ours. You can turn off or delete this data from the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings. If you are not signed in to Play Games, everything stays on your device.

Third-Party Services

Apart from Unity's advertising services, Google Play Billing / Huawei In-App Purchases and Google Play Games described above, the App does not use any third-party advertising or analytics services.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Sharing & Sale

We do not sell your data. Apart from the advertising data collected directly by Unity and the purchase and cloud-save data handled by Google or Huawei as described above, we do not share data with any third parties.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

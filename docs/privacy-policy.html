Retro Arcade — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

Retro Arcade ("the App") is a collection of classic arcade-style games (Brick Breaker, Block Drop, Block Rise, Snake, Tank War, Racing, Star Defender, Memory Match, Sudoku, and Solitaire). This page explains what data the App collects and how it's used.

Data We Collect

  • Advertising ID. The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) to serve and, where permitted, personalize ads. We do not access or store this identifier ourselves — it's collected directly by Google's AdMob SDK. See Google's Advertising Policies for details.
  • Game data (high scores, progress, settings). Saved entirely on your device using local storage. This data is never transmitted to us or to any server we control.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertising. AdMob may collect data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Sharing & Sale

We do not sell your data. We do not share any data with third parties beyond what's described above (Google AdMob, for ad delivery).

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Retro Arcade — Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback? Email wcabdul01@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a few days.

When reporting a bug, it helps to include your device model, OS version, which game you were playing, and what happened.

Common questions

Where are my high scores stored?
On your device only. There is no account or cloud sync, so uninstalling the app or clearing its data resets your scores.

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Every game plays offline.

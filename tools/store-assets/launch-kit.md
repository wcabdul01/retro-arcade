Copy-paste text for promoting Retro Arcade outside Reddit (Reddit posts are
in `reddit-posts.md`).

**Assets** (in `tools/store-assets/`):
- Clips (15-20 s, vertical): `videos/stardefender.mp4`, `blockdrop.mp4`,
  `blockrise.mp4`, `racing.mp4`, `memorymatch.mp4`, `solitaire.mp4`

**Ground rules everywhere**
- Be honest: the app is free and ad-supported (small menu banner, optional
  reward videos). Don't call it "ad-free" or "no ads".
- If a site asks whether AI was used (itch.io does), answer truthfully.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour or two after posting.
- Don't paste the same text in many places on the same day (spam filters).

## 3. itch.io page

itch.io → Upload new project → Kind: **"Games"**. Choose a free/no-download
type that links out (or upload nothing and add the Play link). Fill in:

- **Title:** Retro Arcade: 10 Classic Games
- **Project URL:** retro-arcade
- **Short description / tagline:** 10 classic arcade games in one free, offline app with a retro green-screen look.
- **Classification:** Games · **Release status:** Released
- **Pricing:** No payments
- **Genre:** Action (secondary: Puzzle)
- **Tags (up to 10):** retro, arcade, pixel-art, snake, puzzle, shoot-em-up, brick-breaker, sudoku, solitaire, android
- **AI disclosure:** answer honestly per itch.io's AI-generated content question.
- **Links:** add "Get it on Google Play" → the Play link.
- **Cover image:** `screenshots/final/00-hub.png` (cropped to 630x500)
- **Screenshots:** `01` to `09` from `screenshots/final/`
- **Gameplay video:** upload `stardefender.mp4` to YouTube and paste the link.

**Description:**

Retro Arcade packs 10 classic arcade games into one free Android app, all in a nostalgic 4-shade green handheld style.

🕹️ The games
- Brick Breaker: smash every brick across 5 hand-built levels
- Block Drop: the classic falling-block puzzle
- Block Rise: rows push up from below, so clear them fast
- Snake: eat, grow, don't bite yourself
- Tank War: blast through walls and enemy tanks
- Racing: endless lane-dodging with boost
- Star Defender: hold the line against the invading fleet
- Memory Match: find every pair before time runs out (5 difficulties)
- Sudoku: fill the grid, five mistakes allowed
- Solitaire: clear the tableau, ace to king

✅ Plays fully offline · no account · no sign-in
💸 Free with ads: a small banner on the menu, and optional "watch an ad" rewards (continue after game over, extra hints/undos)

Available now on Google Play.
